# Glossary and Content Style Guide

> **Status:** T0.8 deliverable. **Gates all user-visible copy.**
> Four surfaces — voice scripts, PDF templates, web UI, documentation — in three
> languages. Without one source of truth they drift, and drift in a compliance
> product reads as carelessness. Last updated: 2026-09-09.

## The rule

**One concept, one word, everywhere.** Not "verdict" in the API, "result" in the
PDF and "answer" on the web. Where an internal identifier differs from the
user-facing word, both are recorded below and the mapping is deliberate.

---

## 1 · Terms we use, and the ones we have banned

| Internal / API | User-facing (EN) | Never say | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `verdict` | **result** | "ruling", "decision", "judgement" | We do not decide anything. Those words claim authority we do not have |
| `state` | **outcome** | "status" | "Status" implies a process still running |
| `market_average_rent` | **market average** | "fair rent", "correct rent" | The average is a fact; "fair" is an opinion |
| `max_lawful_rent` | **the most they can charge** | "legal rent" | Plain language. "Legal rent" sounds like a fixed official figure |
| `gap_pct` | **how far below the average** | "discount", "under-market" | Neutral description, no implied bargain |
| `evidence_pack` | **your case report** | "legal document", "filing" | It is not a filing until the resident files it |
| `factual response template` | **a response you can send** | "letter", "legal letter", "draft" | "Letter" and "draft" imply we composed an argument. We assembled facts |
| `notice_served_date` | **when they told you** | "service of notice" | Nobody says "service of notice" on the phone |
| `shortfall_days` | **days short** | "deficiency" | — |
| `review_status: provisional` | **pending review by a qualified lawyer** | "unverified", "beta" | States precisely what is missing |
| `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` | **needs a person to look at this** | "error", "failed", "unavailable" | **It is a legitimate outcome, not a failure** |
| `insufficient_data` | **not enough comparable contracts** | "no data", "unknown" | Names the specific limit |

### Words banned everywhere, in every language

**"advice", "advise", "recommend", "you should", "your rights", "we determine",
"guarantee", "win", "case is strong".**

Each either claims legal authority, predicts an outcome, or converts information
into advice. **The single test:** if a sentence would be improper coming from a
non-lawyer, we do not say it.

## 2 · The three outcomes

Named identically in every surface and language.

| Key | English | Meaning | Tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CLEAR` | **Clear** | The rule settles it on the facts given | Direct. Answer first |
| `CLEAR_WITH_CONDITIONS` | **Clear, with one condition** | Settled, contingent on a named unverified fact | Precise. Name the condition |
| `HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED` | **Needs a person to look at this** | Facts do not permit a determinate answer, or the question is interpretive | **Considered, never apologetic.** This is the system working |

**Never render the third as an error.** Not in colour, not in wording, not in
layout. Doing so teaches the user that honesty is a malfunction.

## 3 · Standing disclosures

Exact wording. Do not paraphrase these — they are the compliance surface.

| ID | Where | English |
|---|---|---|
| `D1` AI identity | Greeting, PDF footer | "I'm an AI." / "Generated by Bayyina, an AI service." |
| `D2` Not advice | Greeting, PDF header | "I give information from Dubai's published rental rules, not legal advice." |
| `D3` Recording | Greeting | "This call's recorded." |
| `D4` Provisional | **At the verdict**, PDF beside the rule | "Our encoding of these rules is pending review by a qualified lawyer, so treat this as information to check, not a ruling." |
| `D5` Not official | PDF, prominent | "This is not an official determination. Bayyina is not a government entity and does not act for any authority." |
| `D6` Caller-stated | PDF, beside inputs | "As stated by the caller. Not independently verified." |
| `D7` Data source | Spoken at verdict, PDF | "Based on registered tenancy contracts published as open data by Dubai Land Department." |

### Surface variants — the only permitted departures

Two disclosures read wrongly on the web. `D2` is written first person because it
is spoken; a web page does not say "I". `D6` says "the caller" because the PDF is
read by a third party; the person at the keyboard is "you", and §5 requires
second person. **These variants are exact strings, not licence to paraphrase** —
anything not listed here must match the table above character for character.

| ID | Surface | Exact text |
|---|---|---|
| `D2` | Web | "Information from Dubai's published rental rules, not legal advice." |
| `D6` | Web | "As stated by you. Not independently verified." |

`frontend/src/i18n/glossary.test.ts` compares the shipped strings against this
file and fails the build on a mismatch. A disclosure that drifts is a compliance
failure, and it is not the kind of thing anyone notices by reading.

**D4 attaches to the verdict, not the greeting.** It concerns the rule encoding,
so it belongs where a rule is stated — which also keeps the greeting inside its
8-second budget. Recorded in `DECISIONS.md`.

## 4 · Legal references — exact forms

Cite identically everywhere. Spoken forms are written out because TTS mangles
numerals in citations.

| Concept | Written | Spoken |
|---|---|---|
| Rent cap rule | Decree No. 43 of 2013, Article 1 | "Decree forty-three of two thousand thirteen, Article One" |
| Notice rule | Law No. 26 of 2007, Article 14 | "Law twenty-six of two thousand seven, Article Fourteen" |
| Tenancy registration | Ejari | "Ejari" |
| Regulator | RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Agency) | "RERA" |
| Authority | Dubai Land Department (DLD) | "Dubai Land Department" |
| Dispute forum | Rental Dispute Centre (RDC) | "the Rental Dispute Centre" |

**Currency:** written **AED 80,000**; spoken **"eighty thousand dirhams"**.
Never "80K", never "Dhs".

**Dates:** written **30 November 2026**; spoken **"the thirtieth of November"**.
Never numeric — ambiguity between conventions produces wrong verdicts.

## 5 · Register

- **Short sentences.** One idea each.
- **No jargon we do not define in the same breath.** "Ejari — your registered
  tenancy contract" on first use in any surface.
- **Second person.** "Your rent", not "the tenant's rent".
- **Active voice.** "They gave you seventy-one days", not "seventy-one days were
  given".
- **No hedging stacks.** "It appears that it may possibly not be permitted" is
  worse than "that increase isn't permitted" — the precision belongs in the named
  conditions, not in the verbs.
- **Numbers spoken as words** in voice; digits in text.

**Reading level target: a competent adult reader in their second or third
language.** That is the actual audience, and it is not a lowered bar — it is a
harder writing problem than writing for a lawyer.

## 6 · Translation

| Language | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| English | ✅ Authoritative | This file is the source |
| Arabic (Gulf) | ⚠ Not started | Legal terms have official Arabic forms — use the DLD/RERA published Arabic, never a fresh translation |
| Malayalam | ⚠ Not started | No official legal register exists; prioritise clarity over formality |

### Rules for translators

1. **Work from this file**, not from the English UI strings.
2. **Disclosures D1–D7 are not paraphrasable.** Translate meaning exactly; have a
   second speaker check them.
3. **Legal citations keep their Arabic official form** where one exists. Do not
   back-translate from English.
4. **The banned-words list applies in every language.** If the target language
   lacks a neutral word for a concept, raise it rather than picking the closest
   authoritative-sounding one.
5. **Numbers and dates follow §4** in the target language's convention, spoken in
   words.

**Machine translation is not acceptable for any string in this file.** A
mistranslated disclosure is a compliance failure, not a typo.

### What ships in the meantime

The web interface **offers only the languages it can actually render**. It does
not show a language button that falls back to English, and it shows no switcher
at all while there is one language (D-065).

This is derived from `_TRANSLATION_STATUS`, so **filling in a locale file and
removing its marker is the whole of the work** — the button appears on its own,
with no component to edit. Arabic and Malayalam remain in `LANGUAGES`, in the PDF
template paths and in the voice scripts, because they are the product: rule 3
above is also the reason Arabic cannot simply be drafted here, since the official
Arabic forms of Decree 43/2013 and Law 26/2007 exist and must be sourced rather
than back-translated.
