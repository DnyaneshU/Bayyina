The whole product turns on the third one. Ink, ground, boundary

Two boundary weights, and the difference is not decoration. Rule separates rows and carries no meaning if unseen. Edge is a boundary a person must be able to find — the outline of a field they have to type into. WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for that; the colour this replaced managed 1.26:1 and was on the rent input.

The scale

One step larger than a typical web scale, with loose leading. The audience is a competent adult reading in their second or third language, on a phone, and often in sunlight — tight leading makes the eye lose its place on a page people read carefully because money depends on it.

44 px minimum on anything tappable. Digits are tabular so a column of rents lines up — comparing them is the entire task. Tab into these to see the focus ring, which is 6.8:1 on both grounds and is never removed.
